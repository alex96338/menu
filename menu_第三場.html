章魚撒鹽脫水，夾進昆布裡冷藏一夜。旨味滲進去，看不見但嚐得到。 The octopus is salted to draw out water, then pressed between sheets of kombu overnight. The savour moves in. You can’t see it, but you can taste it.

盤上的鹽昆布和藏在裡面的是同一種海帶，一個看得見，一個看不見。 The shio-kombu on the plate and the sheets inside the octopus are the same seaweed, one you see and one you don’t.

兩杯是同一支豆，用不同濾杯沖。 Two cups, the same coffee, two different brewers.

蛋白切丁，蛋黃壓泥，分開處理再拌回去。一起壓就變成糊了。 Whites diced, yolks mashed, handled separately and folded back together. Mash them as one and you get paste.

微甜，尾韻有一點黃芥末。那個甜是昭和年代的記號。 Faintly sweet, with a trace of Japanese mustard in the finish. That sweetness is the Shōwa-era signature.

鮮奶油浮在咖啡上，不要攪。糖在奶油裡，不在咖啡裡。 The cream floats on the coffee. Don’t stir. The sugar is in the cream, never in the coffee.
